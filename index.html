Windows & wall TVs: once one exists, its top and bottom heights show as guidelines around the whole house and new ones snap to them (toggle Snap in the dock). Floating table / shelf: no legs — point at a wall to hang it at that height; near a wall it snaps flush, otherwise it stays where you put it. Stacking: any placed item works as a floor — click its top to put something on it. Work is autosaved in this browser and restored when you reopen the file.

Showreel

Builds the house in front of you: floor slabs, then walls with their door and window openings, then doors and windows and stairs, then room by room the paint, wallpaper and flooring followed by that room's furniture. The camera follows each area and never doubles back. Upper floors follow the same order.
